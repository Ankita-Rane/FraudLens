FOLLOW-UP STUDY ARTIFACT VERSIONING POLICY
==========================================

1. A locked, accepted, or previously used artifact is never overwritten.
2. Before replacing a canonical artifact, move the complete old artifact set into a
   clearly named old/ directory.
3. Record SHA-256, original relative path, archive relative path, byte size, status,
   and replacement relationship in an archive manifest.
4. Copy/move operations must be followed by byte-hash verification.
5. Only after archival verification may a newly generated artifact be promoted to the
   canonical filename.
6. Every dependent manifest/protocol must bind the new canonical artifact by SHA-256.
7. Old protocols remain provenance only and cannot authorize a new run.
8. Raw runs are immutable per attempt. Failed or interrupted attempts remain recorded
   and are never silently merged into accepted evidence.
9. Detector studies, panels, attribution evidence, retrieval diagnostics, protocols,
   raw LLM runs, aggregates, analyses, and thesis outputs use separate lineage/status
   fields and must never be pooled across generations without an explicit method.
10. A new artifact hash proves the promoted file's identity; it does not alter or
    invalidate the bytes or hashes preserved in old/.
